Important Notice

EventHub UK acts as a professional event discovery and booking platform. While we aim to present accurate and up-to-date information, specific event delivery, schedules, and final arrangements may still depend on the organiser.

1. Bookings

All bookings made through EventHub UK are subject to availability at the time of request. A booking is considered complete only when the required details have been provided correctly and the booking has been accepted by the platform or relevant event organiser. Submission of a form alone does not always guarantee a final confirmed place where capacity limits apply.

2. Payments

Where payment is required, users must provide accurate and valid information. Ticket prices may vary depending on the event, access level, ticket category, promotional availability, or organiser requirements. EventHub UK reserves the right to correct pricing errors where necessary and to communicate any relevant change before a booking is confirmed.

3. Cancellations & Changes

Cancellation, transfer, and amendment policies may differ across events. Some bookings may be refundable within a stated period, while others may be non-refundable or transferable only under limited conditions. Users should review the event-specific information carefully before completing a booking, especially where premium, discounted, or student tickets are involved.

4. User Responsibilities

Users are responsible for ensuring that the booking details they submit are accurate, complete, and up to date. This includes names, email addresses, ticket selections, and any access or attendance information. Submission of misleading, false, or incomplete information may lead to delays, booking issues, or cancellation where appropriate.

5. Event Changes

Event organisers and EventHub UK reserve the right to adjust event dates, times, venues, speakers, schedules, session availability, or other event details if required by operational, logistical, or unforeseen circumstances. Where possible, affected users should be informed using the contact details associated with the booking, so it is important to keep those details accurate.

6. Liability

EventHub UK aims to provide a reliable platform experience and clear event information, but cannot guarantee that all published details will remain unchanged at all times. Responsibility for final event delivery, venue operations, speaker attendance, and on-site arrangements remains with the organiser. Users should make reasonable checks before travelling or attending where schedules are time-sensitive.

7. Support & Contact

If users have questions about these terms, a booking issue, or platform-related support request, they should contact EventHub UK through the contact page. Support enquiries may include booking clarification, accessibility requests, platform guidance, or general assistance linked to event participation.

Need Help Before Booking?

If you need help understanding ticket options, booking conditions, or event information, our support page can help you get in touch before completing your booking.
