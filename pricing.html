Clear Pricing for Every Type of Attendee

EventHub UK uses a flexible ticket structure designed to suit different attendee needs. Standard tickets offer core event access, premium tickets provide enhanced value, and eligible students can receive a reduced rate based on the standard ticket price.

Simple Pricing Structure

Rather than using one fixed price across the whole platform, EventHub UK applies a consistent ticket model across different events. This keeps the booking experience easier to understand while still allowing each event to reflect its own scale, venue, and access level.

How Student Pricing Works

Student tickets are not treated as a separate unrelated price tier. Instead, they are calculated as a discounted version of the standard ticket price for the selected event. This keeps pricing more logical and ensures that student entry remains clearly tied to the event’s main pricing structure.

Pricing Questions

Why do ticket prices vary?

Each event may differ in venue, scale, organiser requirements, session type, and ticket availability.

Is student pricing always available?

Not every event may offer student access, and some organisers may request proof of eligibility at check-in.

Does premium always include extra sessions?

Premium access varies by event, but typically offers added value such as better seating, priority access, or enhanced networking.
